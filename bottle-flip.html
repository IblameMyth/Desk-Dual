How to play

STAND!
  1. It is lunch break and the bench is free. Take turns flipping a half-filled water bottle onto the desk.
  2. A marker slides along the meter. Tap FLIP (or tap the desk, or press the space bar) to stop it and throw.
  3. Stop it inside the green zone to land the bottle upright. The bright middle is the safest, the edges can still wobble and fall.
  4. The zone moves a little every flip, because the water sloshes differently each time. Watch it before you tap!
  5. Standing = 1 point. The tiny gold zone lands the bottle upside-down on its cap for 3 points, but it is very hard.
  6. Too weak and it lands flat. Too strong and it flies off the desk.
  7. A coin toss picks who flips first. After everyone has flipped, the most points wins. A tie goes to sudden death.
