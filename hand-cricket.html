How to play

  1. Choose who bats first.
  2. The batter chooses a number from 1 to 6.
  3. The bowler chooses a number from 1 to 6.
  4. If both numbers match, the batter is out.
  5. Different numbers add the batter's number to the score.
  6. Each innings lasts until a wicket or 6 overs (36 balls).
  7. The other player chases the target; the higher score wins.
