How to play

  1. Four players receive four secret chits: Raja, Rani, Sipahi (Mantri), and Chor.
  2. Each player checks their own chit privately. Never show your chit to anyone.
  3. After the reveal, every player votes for who they think is the Chor.
  4. The player with the most votes is caught. Ties are decided randomly between tied players.
  5. Raja earns 1,500 and Rani earns 1,200 every round. If the Chor is caught, Sipahi gets 800. If the Chor escapes, Chor gets 800.
