How to play

  1. At the start, everyone votes for a category. The winning category is used for the round. Classmates receive one word; the Behrupiya receives a different word from that category.
  2. Pass the device for each player to privately reveal their secret word.
  3. Discuss the words and decide who sounds suspicious.
  4. Pass the device again so everyone can privately cast one vote.
  5. If the Behrupiya is not voted out, discuss and vote again. If a classmate is wrongly voted out, they leave the round and the remaining players continue.
  6. The Behrupiya wins by surviving until only two players remain.
