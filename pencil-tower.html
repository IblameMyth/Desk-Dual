How to play

DROP!
  1. A classroom item swings on a string above the desk. The desk never moves, the view just zooms out as your tower grows.
  2. Tap DROP (or tap the page, or press the space bar) to let it fall straight down. The dotted line shows where it will land.
  3. A coin toss decides who builds first. That player keeps stacking until the tower crashes.
  4. Every item that stays on the desk is 1 point. When anything falls off, the turn ends and the score is noted.
  5. Then the next player (a friend or the AI) builds a fresh tower. After everyone has played all rounds, the most items wins.
  6. Tip: aim for the middle of the top item. The swing gets faster as the tower grows!
