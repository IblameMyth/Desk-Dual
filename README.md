# 📖 01. THE STORY BEHIND DESK DUAL

Remember those school days?

When a simple wooden desk became a battlefield, a pen became a weapon, and a notebook became an entire gaming arena?

When free periods meant Hand Cricket, Pen Fight, secret chits, and endless challenges with your benchmates?

**Desk Dual brings those memories back to life!**

Desk Dual is a browser-based classroom gaming platform inspired by the games we played during our school days. It recreates the nostalgic atmosphere of an Indian classroom and transforms traditional playground and desk-side games into interactive digital experiences.

From competitive AI challenges to playing with friends, every game is designed to capture the excitement of those unforgettable school moments.

> *"The best memories were never made during lectures. They were made on the last bench."*

# 🎨 04. THE CLASSROOM DESIGN LANGUAGE

Desk Dual isn't designed to look like an ordinary gaming website.

Its visual identity takes inspiration from the classrooms we grew up in.

### 🟩 Chalkboard Green

The classic classroom blackboard inspires the main visual identity.

### 🪵 Wooden Desk Aesthetic

Warm wooden colours and school furniture-inspired surfaces create a familiar atmosphere.

### 📓 Notebook-Inspired Elements

Paper textures, playful details and stationery-inspired visuals reinforce the nostalgic experience.

### ✏️ Typography

The interface uses playful, rounded typography, including:

- **Lilita One** – Bold, expressive headings.
- **Nunito** – Friendly, readable interface text.

### 🎨 Visual Palette

| Colour | Hex | Inspiration |
|---|---|---|
| Chalkboard Green | `#285943` | Classroom blackboard |
| Wooden Brown | `#6E3E1E` | School desks |
| Warm Cream | `#F5E9D0` | Notebook paper |
| Golden Yellow | `#D6A84B` | Pencils and school accents |
| Ink Dark | `#29251F` | Blackboard writing |

*These are the README's visual theme references, not a claim that every value is an exact CSS token in the application.*
