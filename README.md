## 🧑‍🏫 Welcome to Desk Dual!

**Desk Dual** is a nostalgic, classroom-themed gaming platform that brings back the legendary games we used to play with our school friends.

From intense Pen Fights to secret Raja Rani Chor Sipahi rounds, Desk Dual turns your browser into a playground filled with childhood memories.

No homework. No lectures. Just games, competition, and unlimited backbencher energy!

> ✏️ Your desk is your battlefield. Your friends are your opponents. Let the games begin!
