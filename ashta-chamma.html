How to Play

Ashta Chamma is played with four cowrie shells (or tamarind seeds with one painted side) rather than a standard six-sided die.

  • Each shell landing mouth-up counts as 1 and mouth-down counts as 0, so a roll is worth 1, 2 or 3.
  • Two special rolls: 4 “Chamma” (all four mouth-up) and 8 “Ashta” (all four mouth-down).
  • Roll a 4 or 8 to bring a token onto the outer track. Chamma and Ashta come up more often here than in the traditional game, and each gives a bonus roll.
  • Move anti-clockwise around the outer ring. Capture a rival on an unsafe square to send that token home and earn another roll.
  • Capture at least one rival before entering the inner path. Safe squares (X) protect tokens, including the four inner corners.
  • Three bonus rolls in a row forfeit that turn. The first player to bring all four tokens to the center wins.
