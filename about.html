About Desk Duel

Desk Dual is a home for nostalgic classroom games—reimagined for instant play with friends or AI .

🎯 Our Mission

Before high-speed internet and 4K graphics, the best multiplayer games lived on the back pages of notebooks and the corners of wooden classroom desks. At Desk Dual, our mission is to capture that timeless nostalgia and bring it online. Whether you are challenging a childhood friend or warming up against a smart AI, we exist to let you unplug from the noise, feel like a kid again, and share a quick, unforgettable match. Back-bench classics, reimagined for today. Play like a kid again—with friends or AI.
