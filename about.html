ABOUT THE CREATOR

About Desk Duel

Your classroom pen-fighting game — flick your pen, outplay your opponent, and win the match. This page contains the project details adapted from the developer's ExpenseWise About page, presented in the same classroom style as Desk Duel.

🎯 Our Mission

Desk Duel is built to turn the classic desk pen fight into a fun, competitive digital game. Our mission is to make every flick feel satisfying, every match feel fair, and every round feel worth winning — whether you are challenging the AI or battling a friend. We aim to combine simple controls, responsive pen physics, and a playful classroom atmosphere into an easy-to-pick-up game that keeps you coming back for another duel.

✨ Key Features

  • 🖊️ Pen Fighting: Flick and launch your pen across the desk to knock your opponent out of the battle.
  • 🎯 Skill-Based Physics: Use power, angle, timing, and positioning to make accurate shots and outplay your opponent.
  • 🤖 Play vs AI: Challenge computer opponents with responsive gameplay designed to make every duel competitive.
  • 👥 Play With Friends: Take turns and battle another player locally on the same device.
  • 🏆 Best-of-5 Matches: In friend matches, be the first player to win 3 rounds and claim the duel.
  • 🏫 Classroom Theme: Battle on a playful school-desk environment inspired by classic classroom pen fights.
  • ⚡ Quick & Fun Rounds: Simple controls and short matches make it easy to jump in, play, and rematch.

👨‍💻 Developer

Developed with ❤️ as a playful take on the classic pen fight, Desk Duel is continuously evolving with better visuals, smoother physics, new challenges, and improvements based on player feedback.
