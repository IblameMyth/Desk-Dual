How to play

  1. Each player hides six classroom items on a 10×10 notebook page: Ruler (5), Pen (4), Small Pencil (3), Sharpener (3), Rubber (2) and Pen Cap (2).
  2. Items go across or down, never overlapping and never off the page.
  3. A coin toss decides who fires first. Then you take turns calling a square such as B4 or G7.
  4. Tap a square on the enemy page, check the call-out, then press FIRE. A red ✕ is a hit, a blue dot is a miss.
  5. When every square of an item is hit, it is sunk and shown on the enemy page.
  6. Sink all six enemy items first to win. With friends, pass the phone after each shot.
