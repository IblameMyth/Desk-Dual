How to play

  1. An animal name appears on the blackboard, one at a time. You get 2 seconds for each one.
  2. If the animal can fly, tap your UDD! button. Your finger goes up and you shout “Udd!”.
  3. If the animal cannot fly, keep your finger down.
  4. Raise a finger for an animal that cannot fly, or miss one that can, and you are out.
  5. The game ends as soon as only one player is left. The last one on the bench wins, and the blackboard shows everyone from first to last.
  6. With friends, press 1–9 and 0 on the keyboard for players 1–10, or tap your own button.
